### SHOULD-FIX

- **F-1 Focus ring contrast regression on white surfaces (all 38 routes' header + homepage light sections).** `app/globals.css:173-176` sets `outline-color:#FF8FA3` for `.fsc-header :focus-visible` and the new `.fsc-home :focus-visible`. The header is now white (`globals.css:185`) and most homepage sections are white/`#F4F6FA`, so the pink ring is ~2.2:1 against the background, below the 3:1 non-text minimum (WCAG 1.4.11). Previously the override only applied to dark surfaces. Fix: keep the light override for dark panels only (`.fsc-emergency-strip`, `.fsc-footer`, `.fsc-emergency-block`, `.fsc-ir-section`, `.fsc-final-cta`) and let the white header/light sections use the base `#BF0A30` ring (`globals.css:46-50`).
- **F-2 Sticky header hides anchor targets.** `.fsc-header` became `position:sticky;top:0` (`globals.css:185`) with ~116px (desktop) / ~103px (mobile) of strip + bar, and no `scroll-padding-top`/`scroll-margin-top` exists anywhere in `globals.css`. Following `/#maintenance` (header nav, footer, services list, hero) or `#night-view`/`#capabilities` lands with the section heading under the header; keyboard focus moved just below the header is likewise obscured (WCAG 2.2 2.4.11). Fix: `html{scroll-padding-top:<header height>}` or `scroll-margin-top` on `[id]` sections; or drop `sticky`, which the contract does not require.
- **F-3 Hero map does not fit its slot and its labels never get the intended font.** `HeroMap.tsx:72-75` scales Florida to `min(W,H)/3.6` px/degree (desktop) or `slot.w/2.4` (mobile): at 1440×~600 the state projects to ~1155×1017px inside a ~600px-tall hero, and at 390 to ~1010px inside a 350px slot, so only a clipped patch of dots is visible and the state is not recognisable (`S-007-home-1440.png`, `S-007-home-390.png`). Separately, `ctx.font = '700 12px var(--font-sans), sans-serif'` (`HeroMap.tsx:146,151`) and `'500 10px var(--font-mono), monospace'` (`:155`) are invalid canvas font strings (CSS variables are not resolved in `ctx.font`), so the assignment is ignored and every label renders at the default `10px sans-serif` (visible in the screenshots). Fix: fit the state's bounding box to the slot (both breakpoints) and resolve the font family via `getComputedStyle(document.documentElement).getPropertyValue('--font-sans')` before use. The `MI FROM HQ` readout (`:155`) also uses a fixed `0.42` mi/px that is only right for one viewport; derive it from the projection scale (≈69 mi per degree ÷ px per degree) or drop it.
- **F-4 Reduced-motion and off-screen behaviour of the hero canvas.** (a) Under `prefers-reduced-motion`, `HeroMap.tsx:158` draws one frame, but `build()` on `resize`/orientation change (`:99`) resets `cv.width`, which clears the canvas and nothing redraws it — the map disappears. Call `frame()` once after `build()` when `reduce` is true. (b) Without the preference, the ~3,840-dot RAF loop (`:109-160`, counted from the `0.062°` grid) runs for the whole visit, including on touch devices and after the hero has scrolled out of view; gate it with an `IntersectionObserver` (pause when the hero is off-screen) to limit CPU/battery cost on phones and keep Lighthouse main-thread numbers predictable. (c) Under reduced motion the marquee becomes a wrapped grid (`globals.css:313`) that shows all 22 cards, i.e. each logo twice; hide the `aria-hidden` duplicates there (`.fsc-mfr-card[aria-hidden]{display:none}`).
- **F-5 New claims not sourced from the repo — owner confirmation or softening required before merge (C-06: SPEC controls language; contract §4 "no fabricated proof").** None is in the enumerated list (stats, testimonials, licences, ratings, photos, customers), but each states something about FSC's operations or location that no repo content or contract authority line supports:
  - `components/home/HeroMap.tsx:26` labels HQ "HQ · MINNEOLA" and `components/Header.tsx:23` adds "Lake County HQ", while `data/site.ts` records the only verified city as `Clermont` and no street address. Derive the label from `site.address.city` or have Brian confirm Minneola; do not carry two different HQ cities.
  - `app/page.tsx:31` ("installed to UL 325") and `NightView.tsx:13` ("UL 325"): the standard is never mentioned in `data/services.ts`, `data/industries.ts` or `data/resources.ts`. Either Brian confirms FSC installs to UL 325 or the phrase goes.
  - `app/page.tsx:45-48` emergency timeline: "A person answers", "Remote checks where possible — on connected systems we check the operator, controller and network before a truck rolls", "Your manager gets a written service record". These are service-level promises (24/7 live answer, remote diagnostics, written reports). The remote-diagnostics step in particular sits next to the Sentinel/monitoring R&D boundary. Owner must confirm each as current practice or the step copy should be reduced to the approved emergency wording.
  - `app/page.tsx:32` "Add a resident in seconds, revoke one in less" and `:33` "plate readers that turn every vehicle into a searchable record" are product-capability promises; acceptable as marketing if Brian is comfortable, flagged for the same confirmation pass.
- **F-6 Night-view hotspots: keyboard parity and minimum target size.** The six `<button>` hotspots (`NightView.tsx:83`) have no `onClick`; the snapshot action is only on the stage's `onPointerDown` (`:77`), so Enter/Space on a focused hotspot does nothing (focus does reveal the label via CSS, `globals.css:279`). Add an `onClick` that calls the same snapshot path. At 390px the call-box hotspot is 5% × 14% of a 350×380 stage ≈ 17×53px (`NightView.tsx:14`), below the 24px minimum of WCAG 2.2 2.5.8; `tests/release/layout.spec.ts:130` runs axe with `wcag22aa` and fails the suite on serious violations, so give `.fsc-ir-hot` a `min-width:24px;min-height:24px` (`globals.css:272`) rather than relying on the spacing exception.
- **F-7 Evidence gap for AC-2 and AC-6.** `harness/verification.json` registers S-007 with six stages (lint, typecheck, unit, gate, build, e2e), matching contract §3, but AC-2 (`tests/release/contract.spec.ts` sitemap check) and AC-6 (`tests/release/lighthouse.spec.ts` thresholds 90/95/95/95) live in the release suite, which S-006 registered as a seventh stage and S-007 does not. No `S-007-release-*` or Lighthouse report exists under `harness/evidence/`. The 11 manufacturer logos are served `unoptimized` at ~115–192px natural width and `loading="eager"` (`app/page.tsx:84`), which exposes the homepage to the best-practices `image-size-responsive`/offscreen audits for the first time. Run `npm run test:release` on the branch (or add `test:release` to the S-007 stage list) and attach the raw report before merge; until then AC-2/AC-6 are unverified, not failed.
